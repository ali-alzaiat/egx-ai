## 1. Purification calculators: the three incumbent formulas (+ MSCI index-grade variant)

### 1a. Zoya: three named methods with worked formulas (the template for §12)

- **The three methods (official help center):** "Dividend purification method: Purify only dividend income in proportion to the company's percentage of non-compliant revenue. Capital gains purification method: Purify a percentage of capital gains when you sell shares. AAOIFI purification method: Purify the exact amount of non-compliant revenue attributable to each of your shares annually": https://help.zoya.finance/en/articles/8387373-how-do-i-calculate-purification (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Dividend method formula (official blog, worked):** purification = dividend received × non-compliant-revenue %. Steps: (1) read the company's non-compliant-income % from Zoya's compliance report; (2) multiply by dividend; (3) donate. Worked: $100 dividends × 2% = $2 donate, keep $98: https://blog.zoya.finance/stock-purification-guide/ (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Capital-gains method formula (official blog, worked):** purification = (sale price − purchase price) × non-compliant-revenue %. Worked: bought $500, sold $700 → gain $200 × 2% = $4 donate: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **AAOIFI per-share method formula (official blog, worked):** (1) total non-compliant revenue for the financial year (from compliance report → "See breakdown by segment" → toggle %/as-reported amount); (2) ÷ total outstanding shares = non-compliant revenue per share; (3) × shares held = amount to donate annually, **regardless of whether dividends were received or gains realized**. Worked: $5,000 non-compliant revenue ÷ 50,000 shares = $0.10/share; 1,000 shares → $100 donate: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Advisor recommendation (material for §12 default choice):** "Zoya's shariah advisors recommend combining both dividend and capital gains purification" (day traders → capital-gains method; income investors → dividend method; comprehensive → AAOIFI method): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Purification ≠ zakat ≠ rewarded charity (doctrine note for UX copy):** "purification money does not earn any reward, nor does it count towards your annual zakat obligations" (hadith cited: Ibn Ḥibbān from Abu Huraira): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Where the ratio lives in-product:** compliance report screen → "See breakdown by segment" → per-segment toggle between % and as-reported amount: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).

### 1b. Islamicly: dividend-purification ratio with itemized impure-income definition (the other template for §12)

- **Formula:** "Dividend Purification Ratio = Total Non-Permissible Revenue / Total Revenue": https://blog.islamicly.com/halal-investing-guide/purification-of-shariah-compliant-equities (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Worked example:** car-financing interest $2 of $100 revenue = 2% → investor purifies 2% of dividends received: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Itemized impure-income inputs (14-line list — the filing-extraction checklist for EGX):** interest from bank deposits, term deposits, bonds/debentures, money-market instruments, any other interest; non-permissible investment income (equity, derivatives, mutual funds); alcohol, conventional financial services, financial lease, pork, gambling, pornography, tobacco sales, any other identified non-permissible income: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **In-app calculator UX:** per-stock box at the bottom taking "select date" + "Amount of dividends" → instant purification amount + pie chart (Saudi Arabian Refineries example → SAR 1): https://blog.islamicly.com/guide-to-halal-investing/purification-of-shariah-compliant-equities (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Scope doctrine:** purification applies to dividends, not capital gains ("why purification on capital gains is not applicable while purification on income received in the form of dividends has to be purified" — Islamicly's stated position, differing from Zoya's optional capital-gains method): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10). **Design implication:** the product must label which doctrine each estimate follows; the two incumbents disagree here.
- **Egypt surface:** Egypt country page shows green/red verdicts, purification ratios, sector search: REUSED Phase-2 B2 (https://www.islamicly.com/home/Country/Egypt, fetched 2026-10-10). Per-ticker Egypt purification amounts, universe count, update timestamps, methodology version: `no data` (carried over).

### 1d. MSCI Islamic index dividend-adjustment factor (index-grade purification math — citable primary)

- **Formula (MSCI Islamic Index Series Methodology, April 2024, §2.3):** dividend adjustment factor = (total earnings − (income from prohibited activities + interest income)) / total earnings; total earnings = total revenues including interest income; interest = operating + non-operating; updated quarterly as part of Business Activity Screening: https://www.msci.com/indexes/documents/methodology/2_MSCI_Islamic_Index_Series_Methodology_20240403.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10). Identical formula in April 2015, May 2019, August 2012 editions (all fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Fund-level application precedent (purification inside the product, new 2026):** iShares MSCI World Islamic UCITS ETF moves from investor-responsible purification (MSCI calculation) to fund-integrated purification — "any impure dividend monies will be donated by the Fund to one or more charities … Pure and impure dividend amounts will continue to be based on MSCI's calculation" — effective dividends from 1 Jan 2026: https://www.blackrock.com/uk/literature/shareholder-letters/ishares-ii-plc-shareholder-letters-isiswdttl-en-17-oct-25.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Failed-company dividend rule (secondary-market doctrine, useful for §12 edge case):** "To calculate dividend purification for a company that FAILs the rulebook while being held; purify the entire dividend amount, while if it PASSES, only the non-permissible portion": https://ir-boursa.s3.amazonaws.com/faq/FAQ.pdf (IdealRatings/Boursa FAQ, fetched 2026-10-10).

## 2. AAOIFI screening + purification doctrine (citable sources only; full text gated)

- **What is citable vs gated:** The AAOIFI Shariah Standards volume (incl. Standard No. 21 Financial Papers and the in-development Standard on Purification of Impermissible Income) is paywalled/members-only: standards index https://aaoifi.com/standard/Shariah-standards?lang=en and "Shari'ah Standard on Purification of Impermissible Income" listed under development https://aaoifi.com/standards-under-review-4?lang=en (both fetched 2026-10-10). **No AAOIFI full-standard PDF was retrieved; do not quote AAOIFI clause language as primary.**
- **Best citable AAOIFI-threshold source (AAOIFI Secretary-General's own deck):** Dr. Hamed Merah (Secretary General, AAOIFI), OIC Exchanges presentation, Nov 2017 — AAOIFI column: leverage (total debt to trailing-12m avg market cap) <33%; receivables `not applicable`; cash + interest-bearing securities to trailing-12m avg market cap <33%; non-halal income <5%; plus cleansing doctrine: "mixed shares will have to be cleansed (purified)… non Halal Income will have to be donated to Charity and cannot be included in the investors P&L" and "RIBA is not allowed at any circumstances (Not even 1%), BUT … it is allowed to invest … if it passes the Shari'ah thresholds while it's a must to purify income through the Cleansing Process": https://www.oicexchanges.org/files/1---shari-ah-screening-in-the-islamic-capital-markets-dr-hamed-merah-secretary-general-aaoifi.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Zoya's AAOIFI restatement (citable, advisor-guided):** business screen (core prohibited → fail; incidental ≤5% of total revenue) + financial screen (interest-bearing debt AND interest-bearing assets ≤30% of market cap); "See Full Report" exposes exact ratios, thresholds, underlying data; 30% derived from the one-third hadith (Al-Bukhari #5659), rounded down from 33%: https://help.zoya.finance/en/articles/4189798-how-does-zoya-screen-stocks-for-shariah-compliance (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Musaffa's AAOIFI restatement (citable, Amanah-certified):** three ratios — Impermissible Revenue ÷ Total Revenue <5%; Interest-Bearing Assets ÷ Trailing 36-Month Avg Market Cap <30%; Interest-Bearing Debt ÷ Trailing 36-Month Avg Market Cap <30%; certificate MSFA-001 (Amanah Advisors, 20 Apr 2022): https://musaffa.com/screening-methodology/ and https://uat2.musaffa.com/assets/stock-certificate.pdf (both fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Threshold comparison table (citable secondary):** AAOIFI ≤30% mkt-cap debt / ≤30% mkt-cap cash / no receivables test / ≤5% impure income vs DJIM ≤33% 24m-avg-mkt-cap (3 ratios) vs FTSE ≤33.33% assets + ≤50% assets liquidity vs MSCI ≤33.33% assets (×3) vs S&P ≤33% 36m-avg-mkt-cap + ≤49% receivables: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.22858v1 (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Key denominator divergence (design-critical for req 1 versioning):** AAOIFI uses **current market cap**; Musaffa uses **trailing 36-month avg market cap with a 30% threshold** — a hybrid matching no published standard exactly: https://www.halaltradingroom.com/methodology (fetched 2026-10-10). Zoya default AAOIFI uses market cap; Pro offers S&P/MSCI/DJIM/FTSE rulebooks: https://help.zoya.finance/en/articles/4189798-how-does-zoya-screen-stocks-for-shariah-compliance (fetched 2026-10-10). **Our methodology_version must pin denominator + averaging window, not just the standard name.**
- **AAOIFI purification formula (thesis-level citable, not AAOIFI-primary):** "Total Prohibited Income × Number of Shares Owned ÷ Total Number of Shares" (Marmara Univ. Islamic-economics thesis, ch. 2, citing AAOIFI 2004): https://katalog.marmara.edu.tr/veriler/yordambt/cokluortam/5A/68a5bf445c6cb.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10). Same document notes TKBB purifies only if a dividend is received while AAOIFI per-share method applies regardless — the dividend-vs-holding doctrinal fork, citable as secondary.
- **`no data`:** full text of AAOIFI SS21 + Purification standard full texts (paywalled/members-only; tried aaoifi.com index + under-review pages 2026-10-10).

## 3. EGX33 reconciliation: what the official methodology actually says (new retrieval)

- **Official methodology page retrieved (was `no data` in Phase-2):** "The index universe is defined as all equities trading on the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) and domiciled in Egypt, excluding securities that have had 30 or more non-trading days during the three months prior to selection": https://www.egx.com.eg/en/methodologyshariah33.aspx?nav=22 (fetched 2026-10-10 via search-excerpt render; full JS page body not retrieved — deeper clauses still `no data`, see log).
- **Launch + mechanics (official overview):** launched 12 June 2024; base 1 Jan 2022 = 1,000 pts; Shariah criteria approved by a Shariah Committee of Islamic-finance experts; constituents capped at 15% each (fund/ETF-usability motive): https://www.egx.com.eg/en/OverviewShariah33.aspx?NAV=22 (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **S&P collaboration + FRA approval (press, citable):** built "in collaboration with Standard & Poor's, following approval from the Financial Regulatory Authority's main shariah supervisory committee"; universe = EGX100 + companies with a Shariah supervisory board: https://www.businesstodayegypt.com/Article/1/4713/Egyptian-stock-market-launches-inaugural-Sharia-compliant-index (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Review cadence (official press):** semi-annual rebalance effective February + August; Feb-2026 review changed 4 EGX33 constituents; Sep-2026 review changed 2: https://mondovisione.com/_assets/files/press_release_indices_feb_2026_english_english.pdf (2026-01-27, fetched 2026-10-10) and https://www.egx.com.eg/downloads/semi-annual_review_sep_2026_en_english.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Constituent list (official, machine-readable):** ISIN + Reuters + name (ADIB.CA, SAUD.CA, AMOC.CA, ETEL.CA, RMDA.CA observed in render): https://www.egx.com.eg/en/currentindexconstituntes.aspx?type=22&nav=22 (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Threshold discrepancy — reconciliation's central problem:** press summary of EGX33 rules says interest-bearing investments/loans ≤33% of assets-or-market-value, liquid assets ≤70% of total assets, non-compliant income ceiling 10% — "double the standard 5% ceiling … one of the less strict shariah indices" (Asst. If the 10% figure is accurate, a strict-AAOIFI (5%) verdict will fail names EGX33 includes — **reconciliation must display both verdicts side by side, never force agreement.**

Treat as corroboration only — conflicts with the AGBI 10%/70% summary; neither is the official PDF.

## 5. Standing req 1 — versioned verdict: required fields (evidence-backed)

Every verdict must carry: `methodology_version` (standard + denominator + averaging window + threshold set, e.g. `aaoifi-ss21/mktcap-spot/30-30-5` vs `musaffa-hybrid/36m-avg/30-30-5` — §2 divergence), `report_date` (filing date the inputs came from, not the computation date), `as_of`/`computed_at`, and per-rule `UNKNOWN {reason, missing_input}` (Zoya's UNRATED/QUESTIONABLE precedent, §4). Freshness must be per-rule because debt/cash (balance-sheet date) and revenue (income-statement period) can come from different filings. halal.sh's `methodology + as_of + buffer + Pending Analysis` shape (§4) is the closest citable model; Musaffa's month-only stamp is the anti-pattern to beat (REUSED Phase-2 B2 §3).
