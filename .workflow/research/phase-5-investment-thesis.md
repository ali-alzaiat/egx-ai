## 1. SWS Community Narratives — the closest template for §7 (structured thesis + labelled assumptions + stored fair value)

- **What a Narrative is (structured thesis, not a hot take):** "your personal stock insight… Events or trends that could drive the company's growth; Your expectations about how the company or market will behave; Factors that could prevent your assumptions from playing out" + own Fair Value estimate: https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/13416018054415-Getting-Started-with-Narratives (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **5-pillar quality bar (assumption labelling by editorial standard):** "What is the market missing or underappreciating? / How will the business evolve from here to there? / Catalysts, risks, and why this plays out. / Revenue, margin, and earning power impact. / A fair value anchored in your numbers": https://simplywall.st/community/author-program (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **5-question assumption worksheet (the labellable unit to copy):** "What do I think the business will look like in 5 years, and why? / What will revenue and earnings be in 5 years, and why? / Do I agree with analyst growth expectations? If not, why not? / If not, what growth rate should it be? / Based on my growth expectations, is the stock over/undervalued?": https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/7246477551503-Building-Your-Narrative-Around-a-Stock (fetched 2026-10-10). Worked Apple example labels revenue ($500bn, ~5%/yr), gross margin (43%→46% on Services mix 20%→30%), net margin flat on R&D, earnings ($125bn) — each with a "why" pointer (annual report, analyst consensus): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Valuation inputs are labelled and user-set:** Narrative Valuator "adjusts inputs like growth rates or PE ratios to see how the fair value changes"; DCF path labels growth-in-cash-flows (yr 1–10), perpetual growth rate, discount rate (SWS estimate = 5-yr avg risk-free + ERP + levered Beta, e.g. Apple 8.2%); future-multiple path labels future revenue/earnings + exit P/E or P/S + discount-back: https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/13416018054415-Getting-Started-with-Narratives and https://learn.simplywall.st/blog-posts/ways-to-calculate-your-own-fair-value-estimate (both fetched 2026-10-10). REUSED Phase-3 §1: PayPal narrative example "Exit 12x → ~$100 in FY2030, discount 4.5yrs at 10% → $65".
- **Scenario ranges: NOT a native Narrative feature.** Narratives carry one fair value per narrative (bull/bear expressed as separate competing narratives, e.g. "Moving from Science Fiction to Science Fact – A Bullish Valuation Case" vs "Bear Case for…"), filterable Undervalued/Overvalued/Popular, with Fair-Value-history chart vs price: https://simplywall.st/community/narratives and https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/13416018054415-Getting-Started-with-Narratives (both fetched 2026-10-10). No bull/base/bear-within-one-narrative UI observed: `no data` — none on any fetched SWS page 2026-10-10.
- **Invalidation / monitoring (closest incumbent to §9 + §16):** Dashboard/Watchlist/Portfolio + "Important Update" emails surface earnings, dividends, valuation changes, insider selling, news; the instruction is explicit — "look for any developments that potentially validate or invalidate your existing narrative"; sell checklist = (1) narrative changed for the worse? (2) conviction lost? (3) overvalued vs own fair value? — Yes×3 = sell; Q3-only Yes = sell on valuation; Q2-only Yes = trim on conviction: https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/7246565363471-How-to-Monitor-Your-Stocks-and-Discover-New-Investing-Ideas (fetched 2026-10-10). ## 4. How thesis-tracking / invalidation / catalyst products do it (citable, §9 + §16-part input)

- **Professional thesis-ledger pattern (design reference, not a product):** original one-paragraph thesis + 2–4 tracked variables + success criteria (PT with derivation + horizon) + invalidation criteria + exit triggers; evidence classified Confirmed/Pending/Challenged/Invalidated/New; conviction recalibration with sizing action; catalyst calendar per event (date/window + variable tested + base-case expectation + prior Hit/Miss/Pushed); append-only chronological ledger (timestamp, evidence, classification, variable, action, rationale, reviewer); stale-catalyst exception flag: https://www.krasa.ai/industries/finance/skills/investment-thesis-tracker (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Retail falsifiability pattern:** 2–4 pillars, each a claim a real event could confirm/contradict; per pillar the single invalidating fact written while calm (e.g. "Revenue +20% breaks if two quarters <12%"); evidence lives in filings not price (10-K risk-factor changes = early warning); review on new primary evidence, not calendar: https://helmterminal.dev/blog/how-to-track-investment-thesis (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Kill-criteria rules:** every criterion names evidence source + numeric threshold + period ("segment revenue growth <X% for two consecutive quarters as reported in the 10-Q"); trigger forces documented reassessment, not auto-sale; criteria versioned, old preserved, silent removal forbidden: https://thesischeck.io/blog/how-to-use-kill-criteria-investing (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Invalidation-vs-stop-loss separation:** invalidation = fact about the business decided in advance (observable, specific, pre-committed with consequence, e.g. "largest customer at 22% revenue does not renew in FY27 cycle"); 2–4 per thesis; price stop-loss manages risk, says nothing about being right: https://synoptiv.com/insights/what-invalidates-a-thesis (fetched 2026-10-10). Same separation in falsification guide (operating misses / balance-sheet deterioration / competition / valuation triggers; break = review trigger, pre-mortem before buying): https://www.getswoopr.com/learning-paths/stock-investing/thesis-falsification-stock-investing/ (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Catalyst-calendar products (citable, US-led — EGX gap noted below):** Catacal — earnings/IPOs/Fed/macro/product-launch calendar + alerts + weekly top catalysts: https://www.catacal.com/ (fetched 2026-10-10). FinMonkeys Catalyst Radar — real-time SEC (8-K/10-Q/10-K/proxy) feed with AI impact 1–10 + sentiment + confidence + 1D/5D price reaction: https://www.finmonkeys.com/features/catalyst-radar (fetched 2026-10-10). EarningsTracker — SEC-EDGAR-sourced dates, Confirmed (filed) vs Tentative (IR-scheduled) labelling, ICS subscriptions: https://earningstracker.com/ (fetched 2026-10-10). TIKR guide frames the trio: TIKR side-by-side Bull/Base/Bear returns; Alpha Spread auto Worst/Base/Best; Yahoo analyst low/mid/high as baseline: https://www.tikr.com/blog/the-best-free-tools-to-run-scenario-analysis-bull-base-bear-cases (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **EGX-native catalyst/earnings surfaces (thin vs US):** FoudaLens earnings & events calendar exists but rendered no retrievable event rows in this pass: https://foudalens.com/en/earnings (fetched 2026-10-10). Decypha/Mubasher "Calendar of Events" lists dated corporate actions (dividends, results discussions, agenda reviews) per company: https://decypha.mubasher.info/en/stock-market/public/EGX-EGX (fetched 2026-10-10). egypt-stocks.com EGX30 disclosure tracker links earnings releases/statements/AGM/dividend rows per name: http://www.egypt-stocks.com/egx.tracker.html (fetched 2026-10-10). AskBorsa Q2-2026 results tracker (46 of 268 reported, EGP 27.63B combined, per-name YoY%): https://askborsa.com/en/results/q2-2026 (fetched 2026-10-10). No EGX equivalent of Confirmed-vs-Tentative labelling, impact scoring, or ICS subscription observed: `no data`.
- **Seeking Alpha as catalyst-noise caution:** ~20M monthly uniques (2023); crowd theses amplify fast but recycle assumptions; treat as hypothesis source with a verification checklist (filing + math reproducibility + conflict disclosure), never decision authority: https://www.marketdash.com/blog/simply-wall-st-vs-seeking-alpha (fetched 2026-10-10).
