## TRACK A — Phase 2 product research (roadmap §2, §3 + R17 sector backfill)

### A1. The global standard competitors build on: GICS (4 levels, 11 sectors)

- GICS is jointly developed by MSCI and S&P Dow Jones Indices; four hierarchical levels: 11 Sectors, 25 Industry Groups, 74 Industries, 163 Sub-Industries (current structure): https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-gics.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- The 11 sectors are Energy, Materials, Industrials, Consumer Discretionary, Consumer Staples, Health Care, Financials, Information Technology, Communication Services, Utilities, Real Estate: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Each company gets exactly one classification per level, assigned by principal business activity; the 8-digit code encodes the hierarchy (e.g. Sector 25 → Industry Group 2550 → Industry → Sub-Industry): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- GICS is explicitly positioned as the standard used by "asset managers, brokers (institutional and retail), custodians, consultants, research teams and stock exchanges": https://www.msci.com/documents/1296102/11185224/GICS%20Methodology%202022.pdf — via search excerpt (fetched 2026-10-10).
- GICS is NOT assigned to mutual funds, ETFs, sovereigns, shell companies: https://www.msci.com/documents/1296102/11185224/GICS+Methodology+2023.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Implication for Phase 2 exit criteria:** store a 4-level table (`sectors → industry_groups → industries → sub_industries`) mirroring GICS codes + names, rather than inventing a taxonomy. EGX-native 18-sector labels (A2) become a mapping layer on top, not the canonical key.

### A2. EGX's native sector taxonomy (the backfill target — 18 sectors)

- EGX publishes an 18-sector list on its listed-stocks page: Banks; Basic Resources; Health Care & Pharmaceuticals; Industrial Goods, Services and Automobiles; Real Estate; Travel & Leisure; Utilities; IT, Media & Communication Services; Food, Beverages and Tobacco; Energy & Support Services; Trade & Distributors; Shipping & Transportation Services; Education Services; Non-bank financial services; Contracting & Construction Engineering; Textile & Durables; Building Materials; Paper & Packaging: https://www.egx.com.eg/en/listedstocks.aspx (fetched 2026-10-10).
- EGX's daily sector watch reports value/volume/market-cap per sector (e.g. Banks EGP 938.3B cap / 23.62% of total; Real Estate 20.19% of value; Non-bank financials 24.58% of volume — snapshot date on page): https://www.egx.com.eg/en/MarketWatchSectors.aspx (fetched 2026-10-10).
- EGX maintains per-sector index-constituent pages (e.g. Banks index lists 12 constituents incl. COMI, ADIB, SAUD, FAIT/FAITA): https://www.egx.com.eg/en/currentindexconstituntes.aspx?nav=5&type=3 (fetched 2026-10-10).
- EGX30 constituents page (29/09/2026 update) shows CIB weight 29.83%, confirming single-name concentration that makes peer comparison essential: https://www.egx.com.eg/en/currentindexconstituntes.aspx?type=1&nav=1 (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **R17 backfill guidance:** the authoritative source for `companies.sector` backfill is the EGX listed-stocks/sector pages above — free, primary, no auth. Each listing row gives (Company, ISIN, Sector). ISIN is the join key (Reuters `.CA` tickers are secondary). Re-scrape on a schedule; store `source_id` (EGX page URL) + `as_of` per row per AGENTS rule 3.

### A4. Peer-group + peer-percentile patterns competitors use (what Phase 2 should copy and what to avoid)

- **Simply Wall St — peer-relative valuation is the core pattern.** Company valuation pages show "Price-To-Sales vs Peers" and "vs Industry" tables with peer average + per-peer forward multiple, estimated growth, market cap, and an editable peer list ("Edit Peers"): https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/software/nyse-snow/snowflake/valuation (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Simply Wall St — Snowflake model (5 axes × 6 checks).** Each company gets 5 criteria (Valuation, Future growth, Past performance, Financial health, Dividend), 6 pass/fail checks each; Snowflake size/colour encodes total passes; explicitly "NOT a buy or sell recommendation": https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/360001740916-How-does-the-Snowflake-work (fetched 2026-10-10). Independent review notes the fair-value model is a non-adjustable "black box" and data is end-of-day, equities-only: https://www.matchmybroker.com/tools/simply-wall-st-review (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Finviz — peers as a paywalled Elite feature.** Free gets delayed data + basic screener; Elite unlocks "Peers, held by, correlation & inverse correlation," real-time data, 8 yrs financials (vs 3 free), backtesting, API/export: https://finviz.com/elite?utm_source=finviz&utm_medium=banner&utm_campaign=api-and-exports (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Koyfin — dashboards + custom formulas are the peer-comparison vehicle.** Paid tiers unlock "unlimited custom dashboards," "unlimited custom data / custom formulas," "custom financial templates," ETF valuation: https://koyfin.com/pricing (fetched 2026-10-10). Review summary: free = 2Y financials/1Y estimates/2 watchlists/2 screens; Plus = 10Y history + screener + unlimited watchlists: https://simplemarkets.io/blog/post/koyfin-review (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **TradingView — peer context is chart/technicals-led, not fundamentals-led.** EGX coverage exists (EGX:COMI page with price 138.10 EGP snapshot, EGX30 components page listing COMI/TMGH/ETEL): https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/EGX-COMI and https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/EGX-EGX30 (both fetched 2026-10-10). But paywalled value is charts-per-tab, indicators-per-chart, alerts, historical bars — not fundamental peer percentiles: https://www.tradingview.com/gopro (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Yahoo Finance — peer comparison via "compare" + analyst data, best history behind Gold.** Free = quotes/news/basic screeners/manual portfolios; Bronze adds diversification/risk views; Silver adds Morningstar ratings + research reports; Gold adds AlphaSpace + Smart Money Screener + 40+ years financials + CSV export: https://finance.yahoo.com/plus and https://thecollegeinvestor.com/88299/yahoo-finance-plus-review (both fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Design steer for Phase 2 (ownable, honest):** copy SWS's *editable peer list + vs-peers-vs-industry dual comparison* and Finviz's *saved-preset* pattern, but fix their honesty gaps: (1) peers must show `methodology_version` + as-of; (2) percentile must degrade to INSUFFICIENT_DATA when <N peers have data (never rank 1-of-1 as "top decile"); (3) industry metrics must be configurable per industry (banks need NPL/capital ratios, not P/E-only) — no competitor above does per-industry metric sets transparently on EGX.

### A6. EGX33 Shariah index: the only official EGX Shariah taxonomy anchor

- EGX launched the EGX33 Shariah Index 12 June 2024; base 1 Jan 2022 = 1000 pts; constituents capped at 15% each; semi-annual review (end-Jan/end-Jul, effective Feb/Aug): https://www.egx.com.eg/en/overviewshariah33.aspx?nav=22 (fetched 2026-10-10) and academic corroboration: https://acjalexu.journals.ekb.eg/article_450969_17a619b12b6873a553a141b44d46fc17.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Launch coverage: 33 companies across 16 sectors selected from EGX100, incl. TMG, Elsewedy, Abu Qir, Telecom Egypt, Fawry, Orascom Construction, Emaar Misr: https://www.agbi.com/analysis/markets/2024/06/high-hopes-egypt-shariah-compliant-stock-index (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Published thresholds (press summary): interest-bearing investments/loans ≤33% of total assets or market value; liquid assets ≤70% of total assets; non-compliant income ceiling reported as 10% in this article vs the standard 5% — flagged as one of the less-strict indices: same AGBI URL (fetched 2026-10-10). AAOIFI-standard restatement (business halal; debt ≤33% market cap; cash+receivables ≤33%; impure revenue <5%): https://foudalens.com/en/halal-stocks (fetched 2026-10-10, third-party aggregator — treat as corroboration, not primary).
- Full official methodology PDF / Shariah committee names / purification rules: `no data` — EGX overview page links "Index Rules & Methodology" but the document itself was not retrieved in this pass; do not cite thresholds as EGX-official beyond the AGBI summary.
- Constituent list with ISIN + Reuters codes is published: https://www.egx.com.eg/en/currentindexconstituntes.aspx?nav=22&type=22 (fetched 2026-10-10).

### B3. Ownable gaps — framed as advantages, not copying

1. **The versioned Shariah verdict.** Everyone shows halal/doubtful/haram; nobody pins `methodology_version + report_date + per-rule UNKNOWN with reason` on every verdict. Our engine already stores methodology versions — surface it and EGX33-reconciliation becomes a trust moat Musaffa's "As of September 2026" can't match.
2. **EGX-native + GICS-dual taxonomy with provenance.** EGX's 18 sectors (free, primary) × GICS 4-level mapping, each row sourced (EGX URL + as-of). Decypha charges $600/yr for a proprietary filter list; we give the mapping away with citations.
3. **Per-industry metric profiles + honest percentiles.** Banks vs real estate vs industrials get different metric sets, versioned; percentiles refuse to rank when N-with-data is too small (INSUFFICIENT_DATA, never 1-of-1 "top decile"). SWS/Finviz/Koyfin all rank first, explain never.
4. **Data-quality as the product, not a footnote.** Phase 1's quality score (OK/DEGRADED/POOR/MISSING + as-of + last_synced_at) on every company page, screener, and Shariah verdict. No competitor does this; their staleness is a footer or nothing.
5. **Purification math for EGX dividends.** Per-ticker impure-revenue ratios with worked purification amounts in EGP, tied to the same filings the verdict cites. Claimed by Islamicly generically; owned by nobody verifiably for EGX.
6. **Don't build:** real-time charts/alerts (TradingView/Thndr own it), US-stock breadth (SWS/Yahoo own it), execution/custody (brokers own it; rule 9 forbids it), AI chatbot stock-picking (everyone's demo, nobody's moat, AGENTS rules 6/10 restrict it). Monetize research + screening + monitoring; let brokers keep order flow — partner (Thndr/EFG API collaboration later), don't compete.
