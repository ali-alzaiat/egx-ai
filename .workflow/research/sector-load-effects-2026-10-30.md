# Effects of loading EGX sectors (2026-10-30)

225 companies got their EGX sector (ruling R47 S3); everything was then recomputed. Before/after snapshots of the latest Shariah, score and fair-value rows per company (230 companies, junk RT9 rows excluded).

## Shariah (our verdict)
| Status | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Compliant | 0 | 41 |
| Non-compliant | 118 | 119 |
| Insufficient data | 112 | 70 |

- 41 moved from insufficient data to compliant: the industry screen could now run and all financial ratios already passed. Of these, 15 are in the official EGX33 Shariah index.
- 1 moved to non-compliant: SAIB (Banks, conventional) now fails the industry screen.
- Still no industry screen for 30 companies, by design: Food, Beverages and Tobacco (14), Non-bank financial services (8), Travel & Leisure (5), IT/Media (1) are mixed sectors that the activity tags do not clear without a per-company review; 2 have no sector.

Official EGX33 members (34 with a verdict): 15 compliant, 15 non-compliant, 4 insufficient data. The 15 non-compliant members fail our financial ratios (debt ratio 7, interest income ratio 7, cash and securities 1), not the industry screen. This disagreement predates the sector load. It includes ADIB, SAUD and FAIT, Islamic banks that fail the interest-income ratio.

## Valuation and score
No change: 0 fair values moved, no verdict changed, score headline statuses unchanged (226 insufficient data, 4 available). Fair-value profiles stay default (219) and banks (11, detected from statement lines); score profiles stay default. Sector-relative profiles need their own design decision (R47 S1).
