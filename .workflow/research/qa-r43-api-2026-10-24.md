| ID | AC | Sev | Evidence | Location |

Test: `test_dependent_of_an_already_failed_engine_is_marked_failed` (xfail strict). | `app/services/recompute.py:666-677` (`failed` set holds only this-run failures; the fresh-skip at 666 ignores stored `status`) |

Tests: the two xfail params of `test_compute_error_is_200_with_failed_status`. | `app/api/metrics.py:29-93`, `app/api/technical.py:25-50` |
