## 1. SWS Snowflake — composition: 5 × 6 equal-weighted checks, all thresholds public, all components visible

- The Snowflake is a visual summary across **5 assessment criteria** (Valuation, Future growth, Past performance, Financial health, Dividend); per criterion **6 individual checks**, pass = 1 / fail = 0, summed per axis (e.g. Dividend 5/6); axis length encodes the per-criterion total; colour encodes the grand total (red→orange→yellow→green as passes increase); funds/ETFs get a non-comparable blue flake: https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/360001740916-How-does-the-Snowflake-work (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Weights: equal and explicit — every check is worth exactly 1 point; there is no inter-criterion weighting.** Total = 30 checks; no axis is weighted above another in the model doc ("the total successful checks are added"; "the greater the total number of successful checks the larger/greener the snowflake"): https://github.com/SimplyWallSt/Company-Analysis-Model/blob/master/MODEL.markdown (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Only the Value axis is price-sensitive, and deliberately damped ("change must be significant to breach the various thresholds"); the other four axes move only on new company/analyst data (earnings, forecasts, balance sheet, dividend): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Value checks (6) with exact thresholds:** (1) price ≥20% below DCF fair value = "moderately undervalued", 1 pt; (2) price ≥40% below DCF = "substantially undervalued", 1 pt (so a 40%+ discount scores 2/6 on intrinsic alone); (3) PE >0 and < country-market average PE, 1 pt; (4) PE >0 and < industry-average PE, 1 pt; (5) PEG in 0–1, 1 pt; (6) PB >0 and < industry-average PB, 1 pt: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10). Note the help-center Valuation article now describes a P/E→P/S→P/B auto-select + "Fair Ratio" frame (REUSED Phase-3 §1), while the open-source MODEL.markdown still documents PE/PEG/PB — treat MODEL.markdown as the versioned threshold source and the help article as the newer relative-leg description; do not merge them without a version pin.
- **Future-growth checks (6):** (1) forecast earnings growth > low-risk savings rate + inflation premium (or expected-to-turn-profitable in 5y passes), 1 pt; (2) forecast earnings growth > country-market average, 1 pt; (3) forecast revenue growth > country-market average, 1 pt; (4) forecast earnings growth >20%, 1 pt; (5) forecast revenue growth >20%, 1 pt; (6) forecast ROE in 3y >20%, 1 pt. Growth rates fitted by weighted linear regression (analyst-count-weighted, first point = latest reported, weight 1): same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Past-performance checks (6):** (1) 1-yr EPS growth > industry average; (2) EPS now > EPS 5y ago; (3) current-yr EPS growth > 5-yr average EPS growth; (4) current ROE >20%; (5) current ROCE > ROA 3y ago… *(as returned; see PM decision for binding scope)*; (4) current ROE >20%; (5) current ROCE > ROCE 3y ago; (6) current ROA > industry average. 5-yr averages via linear-regression trend on quarterly LTM EPS: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Health checks, non-financials (6):** (1) short-term assets > short-term liabilities; (2) short-term assets > long-term liabilities; (3) D/E ratio not increased vs 5y ago; (4) D/E <40%; (5) operating cash flow >20% of total debt; (6) EBIT ≥5× net interest expense. Loss-makers swap checks 5–6 for two cash-runway checks (stable-rate and trend-rate coverage of next-12-month negative FCF): https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/9812782597135-Understanding-The-Financial-Health-Section (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Health checks, financials (6, separate track — direct precedent for req 3 industry profiles):** assets ≤20× equity; loan-loss allowance > non-performing loans (100%+); deposits >50% of liabilities; net loans <110% of assets; loans <125% of deposits; net charge-off ratio <3%: same URL (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Dividend checks (6):** stable-dividend (no >20% YoY DPS drop over 10y); growing-dividend (DPS now > DPS 10y ago; fails by default if paying <10y); earnings coverage (earnings payout 0–90%, REITs 0–100% on FFO); cash coverage (cash payout thresholds per help article); plus yield-vs-industry/market level checks. Stability/growth/coverage confirmed: https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/8604726406159-Understanding-The-Dividend-Section (fetched 2026-10-10). Exact cash-payout % and yield-comparison thresholds: `no data` — help-article render truncated in this pass; do not assert numbers beyond earnings-payout 90%/100%.
- **Component visibility: full.** Every check is listed with its threshold on the open-source model page and per-section help articles; live company pages show per-axis x/6 scores (e.g. DSV: Valuation 2/6, Future 3/6, Past 0/6, Health 2/6, Dividends 0/6) plus Rewards/Risks lists and a "Data / Last Updated (UTC)" provenance footer (Company Analysis timestamp, EOD price timestamp, earnings dates): https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/transportation/otc-dsdv.y/dsv (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Score-vs-confidence: NOT separated.** A failed check and a missing-data check both just shrink the flake; there is no confidence badge, no INSUFFICIENT_DATA state, no uncertainty ladder (REUSED Phase-2 A4/B2, re-verified on help page 2026-10-10 — "NOT a buy or sell recommendation" disclaimer is the only hedge): https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/360001740916-How-does-the-Snowflake-work (fetched 2026-10-10). Data is end-of-day, S&P Global-supplied, 10yrs+ history, 120K stocks / 90 markets (company-claimed): https://simplywall.st/ (fetched 2026-10-10).
- SWS data-source + staleness disclosure (per-report "View Data Sources", S&P Global attribution footer): https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/software/nyse-snow/snowflake (fetched 2026-10-10).

## 2. Morningstar star rating — composition: 4 inputs, moat inside fair value, uncertainty sets thresholds, full ladder public

- **Four inputs drive the single-point star rating:** (1) economic-moat assessment, (2) fair-value estimate, (3) uncertainty around fair value, (4) current market price: https://www.morningstar.com/content/dam/marketing/shared/research/methodology/705988Morningstar_Equity_Research_Methodology.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Weights: moat is not a separate scored pillar — it enters through the DCF.** Stage II (fade of excess returns toward cost of capital) lasts 1y (no-moat) to 10–15y+ (wide-moat); wide-moat companies compound excess returns longer → higher intrinsic value at identical near-term forecasts. Five moat sources (intangibles, switching costs, network effect, cost advantage, efficient scale); wide = durable >20y, narrow = ≥10y: same URL + https://www.morningstar.com/investing-terms/economic-moat (both fetched 2026-10-10). Capital Allocation rating is analytical context, not a star-rating input: methodology PDF (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Fair value = analyst 3-stage FCFF-at-WACC DCF** (banks/FCFE-at-CoE); multiples are cross-checks only (REUSED Phase-3 §2, re-verified in methodology PDF 2026-10-10).
- **Exact star thresholds (price/fair-value bands) by Uncertainty Rating — the §6 threshold table to copy:** Low: 5★ ≤0.80 / 1★ ≥1.25; Medium: 5★ ≤0.70 / 1★ ≥1.35; High: 5★ ≤0.60 / 1★ ≥1.55; Very High: 5★ ≤0.50 / 1★ ≥1.75; Extreme: 5★ ≤0.25 / 1★ ≥4.00 (i.e. 75% discount / 300% premium). Full 1–5★ worked example for Low ($100 FV → 1★ ≥$125, 2★ $105–125, 3★ $95–105, 4★ $80–95, 5★ ≤$80) and Extreme ($100 FV → 1★ ≥$400, 2★ $200–400, 3★ $50–200, 4★ $25–50, 5★ ≤$25): https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/how-determine-whether-stock-is-cheap-expensive-or-fairly-valued (fetched 2026-10-10) and https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/an-introduction-morningstar-uncertainty-rating (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Uncertainty drivers analysts weigh: operating/financial leverage, sales cyclicality, product concentration, pricing power, ESG/contingent risks (REUSED Phase-3 §2 — Coca-Cola/J&J Low … Tesla Very High … Hawaiian Electric Extreme): https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/how-measure-stocks-uncertainty (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Component visibility: partial.** Star, fair value, moat, and uncertainty are all displayed per name, and the threshold ladder is fully published — but the DCF itself is analyst-owned (no per-assumption breakdown for retail) and uncertainty is analyst judgment (quant start = trailing-12m return volatility, analyst overlay decides): https://www.morningstar.com.au/s/documents/MCA_GlobalEquityReportDisc.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- **Score-vs-confidence: separated by construction — the cleanest precedent for roadmap §6 "confidence separate".** Uncertainty never changes the fair value; it only widens the discount/premium required for each star. Worked pair: General Dynamics ($300 FV, Low → buy ≤$240 / sell ≥$375) vs LPL Financial ($311 FV, High → buy ≤$186.60 / sell ≥$482.05) — near-identical values, radically different action points: https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/how-determine-whether-stock-is-cheap-expensive-or-fairly-valued (fetched 2026-10-10). Star recalculated at every market close on price movement alone: same URL. Quant variant adds explicit Not Rated (price stale ≥30d) and Under Review (corporate event / price stale 7–30d / FV ratio outside 0.25–4) states plus ±5% breakpoint buffers — the closest incumbent to our INSUFFICIENT_DATA + anti-flicker design: https://s25.q4cdn.com/634000362/files/doc_downloads/1/Quantative-Equity-Research-Effective-2-Dec-2024.pdf (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Star-rating distribution is NOT forced (5★ count floats with market valuation): https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/how-determine-whether-stock-is-cheap-expensive-or-fairly-valued (fetched 2026-10-10).
- Morningstar EGX coverage (analyst-followed EGX names): `no data` — no EGX-domiciled stock with analyst FV + uncertainty + star retrieved in this pass; do not assert Morningstar covers COMI/TMGH.

## 5. Standing req 3 → §6 mapping (industry profiles feed score confidence)

- Only SWS has a per-industry component split (separate 6-check health track for financials; REIT/AFFO + bank/excess-returns DCF routing; P/B preferred for asset-heavy/financials) — but it is hard-coded per company type, not a versioned configurable profile: https://github.com/SimplyWallSt/Company-Analysis-Model/blob/master/MODEL.markdown + https://support.simplywall.st/hc/en-us/articles/9812782597135-Understanding-The-Financial-Health-Section (both fetched 2026-10-10).

No published per-industry method dictionary: `no data` (carried over Phase-3 §8).
